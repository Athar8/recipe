Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Are the prices final?

No. Prices shown here are sample portfolio-demo estimates. A real repair business should replace them with verified prices and confirm the final quote after inspection.

Do I pay online?

No. This demo does not take payments or store customer details.

Can I use this for my own repair shop?

Yes. Update the business name, services, prices, contact number and location before publishing it for a real business.

How does the booking button work?

After you configure the WhatsApp number in the JavaScript settings, the form opens WhatsApp with a prepared message.
