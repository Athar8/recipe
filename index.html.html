How I work

From first call to launch

A short, clear process so you always know what happens next and what it will look like before it goes live.

  1. Talk

    We discuss your shop, your customers and what you want the site or app to do.

  2. Design

    I share a design for your approval, so changes happen before any code is written.

  3. Build

    I build it for phones first, test it on different screens and keep it fast.

  4. Launch and support

    I put it live, show you how to update it and stay available for changes.
